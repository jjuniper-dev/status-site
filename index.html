03 · GREP-ExP as Agentic AI Reference
What GREP-ExP Actually Is
Global Repository of Epidemiological Parameters – Extraction of Parameters is an LLM-enabled systematic literature review pipeline supporting WHO GREP.
  • Jointly owned by EPSGH/SPIB and DAS/SDSE
  • Automates research screening and parameter extraction
  • Already onboarded to HAIL
  • Accepted in Cochrane Evidence Synthesis and Methods (2025)
Why It Matters to EA
GREP-ExP is one of the clearest current PHAC examples of agentic AI in a governed public-health context.
  • Multi-agent disagreement pattern, not self-confidence scoring
  • Human-in-the-loop retained for adjudication
  • Strong L3/L4 operational AI capability example
  • Useful reference case for ARB and AI capability mapping
