💡 Demo Tips

  • Start with a high-level question: "What does AI governance look like at scale?" Point to Control Plane.
  • Then dive into decisions: "What's blocking production move?" Opens Decisions page. Follow the links to Intelligence for context.
  • Show the assessment workbench: Vendor comparison is visually impressive. Demonstrates decision-support capability.
  • Emphasize living document model: Intelligence page updates in real-time; no reboots or batch processes.
  • Close with operational readiness: Settings page shows governance isn't just policy—it's technical infrastructure too.
  • Time box it: 5 minutes total = 50 seconds per page. Don't drill too deep; stay at 10,000-foot view.
